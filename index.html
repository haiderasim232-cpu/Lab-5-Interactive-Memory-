About This Journey

This 75-second soundscape imagines a day in Lahore. It begins with a quiet morning, moves into the energy of busy streets, and ends in a greener, quieter space filled with evening sounds.

The sounds represent the atmosphere of Lahore. They are existing ambience recordings, rather than recordings verified as having been made in the city.
